What Is RCS Messaging and How Does It Work?

By PS Message India Editorial Team November 2026 5 min read

For decades, default mobile text messaging was limited to short plain-text strings and raw web links. Today, RCS Messaging (Rich Communication Services) transforms the native smartphone Messages inbox into an interactive, media-rich communication channel where brands can present visual cards, product carousels, and one-tap buttons.

1. What Is RCS Messaging?

Rich Communication Services (RCS) is a next-generation communication protocol that upgrades the default Messages app on compatible smartphones. Rather than requiring customers to download a separate brand application, RCS brings rich visual formatting and interactive UI components straight into the native messaging app that comes pre-installed on the user's device.

2. How Does RCS Business Messaging Work?

When a business registers an RCS brand profile and sends a campaign through an enterprise messaging provider like PS Message India, the message is delivered over mobile data or Wi-Fi to RCS-capable devices:

  • Branded Sender Identity: Instead of seeing an unfamiliar alphanumeric string, the customer sees your official business name, brand logo, and brand colour theme at the top of the conversation.
  • Rich Media Rendering: High-resolution banners, product photos, and short videos render natively inside the message card.
  • Interactive Customer Actions: Customers can tap suggested reply chips or action buttons without typing a single word.

3. Rich Cards, Carousels & Action Buttons

The standout strength of RCS Messaging lies in its structured visual components:

Standalone Rich Cards

A Rich Card combines a visual header (image or video), a bold headline, descriptive copy, and up to four interactive buttons in a clean, self-contained card layout—ideal for event invitations, service announcements, or digital boarding passes.

Swipeable Rich Carousels

A Rich Carousel links up to 10 individual Rich Cards horizontally. Customers can swipe left and right inside the message thread to compare retail products, holiday packages, course offerings, or financial plans.

Suggested Actions & Replies

Action buttons can open a specific product page in the browser, dial your customer care number, or open a store's location on a map with a single tap.

4. How RCS Complements Traditional SMS

Rather than viewing RCS and SMS in isolation, modern enterprises use them together as part of a unified communication strategy:

  • Use RCS Messaging when you want visual storytelling, multi-product carousels, and interactive tap buttons on data-connected smartphones.
  • Use Bulk SMS when you need universal offline delivery across every mobile handset for OTPs and instant alerts.
  • Combine with Bulk WhatsApp and Voice Calls to reach every segment of your audience on their preferred channel.

5. Getting Started With RCS in India

Launching RCS Messaging begins with setting up your brand agent profile—including your logo, brand colours, and business details—followed by designing clean Rich Card or Carousel templates for your campaigns.
