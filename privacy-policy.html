Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 2026

At PS Message India ("we", "our", or "us"), accessible from https://psmessageindia.com, we respect the privacy of our website visitors and business clients. This Privacy Policy outlines the types of information we collect and how we use and protect it.

When you submit an enquiry or request a quote on our website, we may collect the following business contact details:

We use the information you provide strictly for legitimate business purposes, including:
