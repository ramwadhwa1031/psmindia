Who We Are

Built Around Clarity, Reliability & Customer Reach

In today's fast-moving digital landscape, every interaction between a business and its customers carries weight. Whether it is a critical one-time password, an order dispatch notification, a seasonal promotional announcement, or an interactive product showcase, timely delivery and message clarity define how customers perceive a brand.

At PS Message India, we focus strictly on four foundational business communication channels—Bulk SMS, Bulk WhatsApp, Voice Calls, and RCS Messaging. By specializing in these core channels, we help organizations simplify their outreach while improving customer responsiveness.

  • Focused suite of four proven enterprise messaging services

  • Practical support for DLT compliance and template organization
  • API integration support within relevant communication channels
