Interactive Native Messaging

Bring App-Like Experiences Inside the Default Mobile Inbox

Rich Communication Services (RCS) is the modern evolution of mobile messaging. Unlike traditional plain-text SMS, RCS delivers rich media, branded sender profiles, interactive carousels, and suggested reply buttons directly inside the user's native Messages application—without requiring them to install any separate app.

With PS Message India's RCS Messaging solution, businesses can showcase products visually, guide customers with one-tap action buttons, and build instant brand recognition in every message thread.

  • Rich Cards & Carousels: Present up to 10 swipeable cards in a single message with images, titles, and descriptions.
  • Interactive Action Buttons: Enable customers to open websites, dial phone numbers, or share locations with one tap.
  • Images & Rich Media: Include crisp banners, product photos, and promotional videos inside the message card.
  • Branded Sender Identity: Display your company name, brand logo, and colour theme at the top of the conversation.

Key Features of RCS Messaging

Designed to maximize visual appeal, brand trust, and customer interaction.

Rich Cards & Carousels

Display standalone rich cards or horizontal swipeable carousels that let customers browse multiple products, plans, or properties side by side.

Suggested Actions & Replies

Include interactive buttons that open URLs, initiate phone calls, open map locations, or send instant pre-filled customer replies.

Branded Business Profile

High-Resolution Media
